## Evidence limits

- P01 and P02 have recording-backed transcripts. P03–P05 have observation notes only.
- Exact paper-tool discovery times were not captured in the supplied notes, so the plan's 15-second threshold cannot be independently verified.
- Required paper strokes were completed, but defect counts were not recorded. The plan's exact 90% reliability threshold therefore cannot be recalculated from raw counts.
- All sessions used physical VR controllers. Simulator-only `T`/`Y` controller selection remains outside this participant dataset.
- The sample was small and drawn from an in-class convenience group. The findings validate this prototype workflow for these sessions, not long-term learning effectiveness.
